Christopher Bindig

Urbanist based in Berlin. In public administration I work on how urban development is communicated, negotiated and organised digitally.

Alongside: visual research with the camera, essays on cities, culture and power, and the question of what data and AI do to public institutions and the public sphere.

Trained in infrastructure management (Stuttgart) and spatial planning (Vienna).

The question I am pursuing

How do people in a more complex, individualised and technologised world come to experience the world around them as something they can shape?
